# AirLearn AI – Future Science Studio

Interactive Arabic science lab for Zayed Educational Complex – Al Ragayeb.

## Included labs
- Earth interior: zoom, rotate, interactive cutaway and layer descriptions.
- Solar system: explore eight planets and their facts.
- Living cell: educational 3D cell and organelles.

## Gesture controls
- Index-finger movement rotates the model.
- Pinch distance adjusts zoom.
- Open palm pauses manipulation.
- Mouse dragging and wheel work without a camera.

## Camera privacy
- Camera is activated only after clicking **تشغيل الكاميرا**.
- Frames are processed locally by MediaPipe Hand Landmarker.
- **إيقاف الكاميرا** stops all stream tracks.
- Camera is stopped when the page is hidden or closed.
- No video recording or upload is implemented.

## Deployment
GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Build and deployment > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
Then visit: https://zidiz08-dot.github.io/airlearn-ai/

## Requirements
Latest Chrome or Edge, webcam, HTTPS and internet to load Three.js, MediaPipe and the hand-tracking model.

## Notes
This is a student educational prototype. 3D models are illustrative and are not scientifically to scale.
